One eye on the wire. No blind spot it won't admit to.

A unidirectional-first passive NDR for data-diode enclaves that treats the unseen return half of every conversation as an inference target — not missing data. 01

The constraint everyone else routes around

Critical-infrastructure operators watch their gateways through a hardware data diode: traffic is copied one way into a monitoring enclave that has no physical path back. It removes a whole class of pivot attack and preserves a clean chain of custody — but the intelligence layer inside can only ever observe. No probes. No completed handshakes. No mitigation pushed back across the wire.

Off-the-shelf detectors were all architected bidirectional-first. They secretly assume a round trip — the SYN-ACK, the request/response ratio, the RTT. On a diode you often see only one direction of the link, and that assumption quietly breaks: connection state never completes, biflow ratios read empty, RTT is unrecoverable. Halving a two-sided model doesn't half-work. It fails.

The missing return half is not missing at random. Its very absence is the signal.

02

The bet: infer the half everyone else throws away

TCP leaks the far side into the headers you do see. The cumulative-ACK field is a running receipt for bytes the peer sent back. The TCP-timestamp echo is a keyhole into the peer's clock. Retransmit backoff tells you whether anyone is even answering. From one direction, CYCLOPS reconstructs a phantom bi-flow with a calibrated uncertainty band — then detects where one-way visibility cannot hurt it.

03

Eight cores that only make sense one-way

The moat is an inference-and-honesty layer no bidirectional NDR has, because none assumed the return half is gone forever.

GHOSTFLOWhalf-flow reconstruction

Cumulative-ACK deltas recover the exact byte volume the peer sent back; a shadow TCP state machine tracks the unseen peer's ISN and connection state. Output: a per-flow reconstructed return series with a variance band — a phantom bi-flow, not a dropped column.

Why it lands: reverse-byte estimates land within a few % of withheld ground truth when ACKs are visible — the constraint becomes a measured signal.
PULSEliveness oracle

RTT and "is the peer actually answering?" with no round trip: the TCP TSecr echo carries the unseen party's clock; SYN-retransmit RTO backoff fingerprints a silent peer; a visible third-ACK proves the server responded even when its packets never crossed the tap.

Why it lands: structurally defuses the #1 one-way false positive — benign asymmetric routing masquerading as half-open floods.
VANTAGEwhich-half routing

Which half you see (client→server vs server→client; victim ingress vs egress) is a latent variable that flips the whole strategy. A classifier infers it from SYN/ACK directionality, port roles and ACK-progression, then routes each threat to a detector bank built for that vantage.

Why it lands: an explicit architectural stance no commercial NDR takes — the same enclave works on either direction of the tap.
SPECTERspectral beaconing

C2 beacons as a point process of egress flow-initiations, scored with a Lomb-Scargle periodogram (built for gappy, unevenly-sampled series) plus phase-fold confirmation. The periodogram's analytic false-alarm probability is the confidence score.

Why it lands: recovers periodicity from timestamps alone — no SYN-ACK, RTT or completeness needed — and survives Cobalt-Strike jitter.
BABELDNS language + covert-capacity

A char-level model + benign perplexity for arithmetic/hex DGAs, a cross-host WordGraph for dictionary DGAs, and the Paxson information bound metering covert-channel throughput for tunnels — asserting a live tunnel exists from poll cadence alone.

Why it lands: emits a real number — "~N kbps of exfil on domain X" — not a binary hunch. Evasion by encoding still surrenders bits the capacity bound catches.
BACKLOG ORACLEDoS mechanism, not a rate

A Little's-Law estimator turns one-sided SYN completion deficits into a live "SYN-RECV backlog % full / ETA-to-exhaustion" gauge, and detects the victim's own SYN-cookie regime-flip as passive confirmation the queue already overflowed.

Why it lands: maps onto the actual DoS mechanism (queue exhaustion), turning the defender's kernel behaviour into an indicator no baseline uses.
CALIBERhonest confidence

Conformal prediction gives every alert a distribution-free coverage guarantee, and HalfSight's reconstruction variance is propagated in — the band widens when a detection leans on an imputed half. Alerts separate Observed / Inferred / Assumed with per-feature TreeSHAP attribution.

Why it lands: for an enclave that can never probe to confirm, calibrated auditable confidence is the deliverable.
WIRESEALcustody + federation

The diode becomes the first cryptographic link of the chain of custody: WORM enforced by physics. Every alert seals into a Merkle-rooted, HSM-signed bundle anchoring the inference chain — model hash, features, thresholds, reconstruction — with roots checkpointed to a permissioned ledger.

Why it lands: a probabilistic one-way alert becomes bit-for-bit re-derivable and court-admissible; two enclaves attest a bi-flow event without shipping raw PCAP.

Why not just run Zeek?

SystemOn a one-way tap
Zeek / Corelightconn.log is biflow-centric — the resp_* half is empty, so flood/scan/exhaustion analytics that lean on bidirectional ratios go silent or degrade.
Suricataa stateful signature/stream engine that often can't complete flow state or fire dual-direction rules on a diode; its model is match-what-you-see, not infer-what-you-can't.
RITAdoes genuine beaconing, but consumes Zeek's bidirectional logs with interval/FFT methods that assume near-uniform sampling.
Darktrace / NDRbidirectional behavioural baselines, typically expect both directions (sometimes active enrichment), with opaque, non-reproducible confidence.
CYCLOPSUnidirectional-first. Reconstructs the missing half as a shared feature layer, conditions on vantage, propagates reconstruction uncertainty into a coverage-guaranteed confidence, and makes each alert reproducible and admissible — the honesty layer none of them have.
07

Honest about scope

what we do not claim to have invented

DGA lexical scoring, beacon periodicity, entropy-based tunnelling and volumetric flood detection are table-stakes. Our contribution is the unidirectional-first inference-and-honesty layer around them — and we route around our own limits with explicit graceful degradation rather than overselling.

QUIC / HTTP-3erodes the ACK/timestamp tricks → fall back to timing-and-volume features.
DoH / DoTblinds lexical DNS features → beaconing-and-volume heuristics + JA4+ fingerprints.
Sampled sFlowdisables per-packet reconstruction → volumetric/entropy detectors only, never faked spectra.
