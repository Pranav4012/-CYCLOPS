CYCLOPS on labeled one-way traffic

Every figure below is produced by eval/run_eval.py over seeded, ground-truth, strictly one-directional traffic — benign load plus overt and low-signal stealth variants of all four threat classes. The detection maths (spectral periodogram, cumulative-ACK reconstruction, split-conformal intervals, Merkle proofs) is computed, not mocked — the pipeline ingests real .pcap captures (parsing genuine Ethernet/IP/TCP/UDP/DNS bytes), and the DGA core is an ML model trained on 337k domains across 25 real DGA families vs real Alexa/OpenDNS legit.

03

CALIBER — coverage tracks the guarantee

Split-conformal prediction intervals on the GHOSTFLOW reconstruction. Calibrated at level 1−α, the empirical coverage on held-out flows tracks the nominal guarantee almost exactly (marginal over 200 random splits).

This is the honest counterpart to a passive system that can never probe to confirm: the confidence band is a distribution-free guarantee, not a vibe.

05

SPECTER — jitter robustness (0% Poisson false-positives)

15 beacons observed 40 beacons observed
Detection scales with observation, as any spectral method must: 15 beacons resolve up to ~20% jitter, 40 beacons up to ~30%. Crucially, aperiodic (Poisson) traffic is flagged 0% of the time after the look-elsewhere correction — a naive periodogram false-fired ~90%. Recovered period error stays under 1% wherever a beacon is detected.

A character-n-gram MLP trained from scratch in numpy on the chrmor DGA dataset — 337k domains across 25 real DGA families (conficker, gozi, matsnu, necurs, ranbyus, suppobox, tinba…) vs real Alexa / OpenDNS legit. ~2× the hand-tuned heuristic's recall.

Honest per-family, straight from real data: arithmetic families are caught at 95–99%, but dictionary DGAs (nymaim, suppobox, matsnu) are the known-hard case — a per-domain char model can't separate real-word concatenations; they need BABEL's cross-host WordGraph. We show the gap. Because an infected host cycles through many rendezvous domains, per-host detection is near-total even where per-domain recall dips.

07

Graceful degradation under encrypted transport

We're honest about where one-way visibility hits its limits — and measure it. When traffic is encrypted the pipeline degrades on purpose rather than fabricating detections.

TransportLexical DGA / tunnelBeacon timing (SPECTER)GHOSTFLOW recon
DoH (DNS-over-HTTPS) encrypts the qnames, so BABEL's lexical detectors go blind — but the beacon's timing is still visible and SPECTER catches it. QUIC/HTTP-3 removes the TCP ACK/timestamp signals, so GHOSTFLOW/PULSE fall back to the prior, yet beacon timing survives. Sampled sFlow (not shown) similarly disables per-packet reconstruction while volumetric/entropy detectors keep working. Measured by eval.run_eval §8.
Reproduce: cd reference-impl && python3 -m eval.run_eval 30 → writes eval/results/metrics.json. Seeded & deterministic.
Run on a real capture: python3 -m eval.make_pcaps && python3 -m halfsight.ingest pcaps/mixed.pcap — parses genuine pcap bytes.
Retrain the DGA model: ./datasets/fetch_datasets.sh && python3 -m eval.train_dga — 25 real families, held-out test.

Honest scope: these are the four in-scope threats on synthetic lab traffic; the harness is a reference sized for clarity, not a NIST-grade benchmark. Baseline comparisons vs Zeek/Suricata/RITA are architectural (they consume biflow features a diode does not provide), not head-to-head numbers.
